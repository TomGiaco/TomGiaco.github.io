I am a PhD student in Computer Science at [Bocconi University](https://www.unibocconi.it/) in Milan, where I work on AI solutions for understanding gene regulation. My PhD is funded by a scholarship from the Fondo Italiano per la Scienza (FIS) for the DECODE project, supervised by [Prof. Andrea Tangherloni](https://andreatangherloni.com).

Before starting the PhD, I completed an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and a BSc in Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence, both at Bocconi. Since 2024 I have been part of [Prof. Francesca Buffa](https://cs.unibocconi.eu/faculty/francesca-buffa)'s group in the Department of Computing Sciences. In 2026 I spent several months as a visiting researcher at the [Weatherall Institute of Molecular Medicine](https://www.imm.ox.ac.uk/), University of Oxford, working in [Prof. Jim Hughes](https://www.rdm.ox.ac.uk/people/jim-hughes)'s lab on epigenomics and gene regulation.

Research interests
======
My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning** and **computational biology**. I am interested in how modern deep learning architectures can be used to understand the complex regulatory logic of the genome, and in what those models can tell us about disease.

* **Gene Regulatory Network inference.** I develop methods that reconstruct the interactions between transcription factors and their target genes from single-cell data. I explore a range of methodologies, from statistical approaches to deep learning models.

* **Sequence-based models of gene regulation.** I work with pre-trained transformer models of the genome, such as Enformer and AlphaGenome, and study how their learned representations can be used to infer cell-type-specific regulatory relationships.

* **Cancer biology.** Most of my work is motivated by cancer research, where identifying dysregulated gene programs can give insight into how a tumour progresses and where it might be vulnerable.

* **Tooling for computational biology.** I believe methods are only useful if other people can run them. I am the lead developer of [`pysigscore`](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.04.742537), a Python package for gene signature scoring across bulk and single-cell transcriptomics.
