Presentation of my work on enrichment-based methods for Gene Regulatory Network reconstruction, extending the CellOracle framework with single-cell RNA-seq and ATAC-seq data to improve the biological interpretability of the inferred networks.

Joint work with Saveria Mazzara, Badran Elshenawy, Andrea Tangherloni and Francesca M. Buffa.
